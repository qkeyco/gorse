# Contributing to Gorse

Outside contributions are welcome: bug reports, rule improvements, fixes and
documentation. Issues can be opened at any time.

## The contributor licence agreement

Before a pull request can be merged, every contributor must agree to the
Gorse Contributor Licence Agreement in [CLA.md](CLA.md).

- **Individuals** agree on their first pull request, from the GitHub account
  the contribution comes from, either by ticking the box in the pull request
  template ("I have read the Gorse Contributor Licence Agreement version 1.0
  (CLA.md) and I agree to it") or by posting a comment with the same words.
  Either counts as your signature (see "How to sign" in CLA.md).
- **Organisations** email support@gorse.is for the organisation version,
  which someone authorised to bind the organisation signs once for all its
  contributors.

### Why we ask

Gorse's source is published under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0,
and QKey Co. also licenses Gorse commercially. To include your work in both,
QKey needs the copyright licence in section 3 and the patent licence in
section 4 of the agreement. You keep the copyright in what you write; the
agreement grants licences, it does not transfer ownership.

## Security reports

Please do not report security problems in public issues. Email
support@gorse.is instead.
